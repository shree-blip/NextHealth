# SEO Sprint Plan — Master Document

**Site:** thenextgenhealth.com
**Sprint Plan Date:** 8 April 2026
**Planning Horizon:** 12 months (April 2026 – March 2027)

> This master sprint plan translates every blueprint in Phase 4 into a prioritised, time-boxed execution roadmap. All supporting rollout plans are in companion documents within this folder.

---

## Executive Summary

### Current State
- **44 indexed pages** across 17 core + 13 services + 7 blog + 7 news
- **Site SEO Score:** 2.66 / 5 (from Phase 3 gap analysis)
- **Critical Gaps:** Zero structured data, zero industry pages, zero automation sub-pages, zero case study detail pages, zero city/location pages, thin content on most pages, weak internal linking, no off-page programme

### 12-Month Targets

| KPI | Current | 3 Mo | 6 Mo | 12 Mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total indexed pages | 44 | 70+ | 110+ | 150+ |
| Organic sessions / month | Baseline | +40% | +120% | +300% |
| Keywords in top 10 | ~5 | 30+ | 75+ | 150+ |
| Domain Rating | ~18 | 25 | 32 | 40+ |
| Site SEO score | 2.66/5 | 3.5/5 | 4.0/5 | 4.5/5 |
| Conversion rate (organic) | ~1% | 2% | 3% | 4% |

## Sprint 9: Compound Growth & Refinement (Months 10–12)

### Focus Areas

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content production bottleneck | High | High | Pre-approve content briefs 2 weeks ahead; maintain 4-week content buffer |
| Developer bandwidth conflicts | Medium | High | Batch technical tasks into sprints; use reusable components |
| Google algorithm update | Medium | Medium | Follow white-hat practices only; diversify traffic sources |
| Competitor catches up | Medium | Medium | Monitor competitors quarterly; maintain content velocity advantage |
| Client data unavailable for case studies | Medium | Medium | Anonymise case studies; use aggregate data; get permissions early |
| Low link-building conversion rates | Medium | Low | Test multiple outreach templates; diversify channels |
