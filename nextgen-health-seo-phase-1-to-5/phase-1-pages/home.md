# Home Page

**URL:** `https://thenextgenhealth.com/`
**Page Type:** Landing / Hub
**Status:** Live

---

## Purpose

Primary entry point. Establishes brand positioning, showcases services, industries, results, pricing, and funnels visitors toward lead capture.

## Current H1

"We market healthcare. Relentlessly."

## Summary of Current Content

The homepage positions The NextGen as a premium healthcare marketing agency targeting ERs, MedSpas, and urgent care centres. It highlights 500+ campaigns launched, $10M+ ad spend managed, and 3× average ROI. Sections cover industries served, services offered, a five-step process (Discovery → Scale), case study results, pricing tiers ($5K/$10K/Custom), blog and news previews, testimonials, and an FAQ.

## Current Primary Topic

Healthcare marketing agency for medical practices in Texas.

## Current CTA

"Get a free strategy call" → links to `/contact`

---

## Internal Linking Observations

- Links to `/services`, `/industries`, `/contact`, `/blog`, `/news`, `/pricing`, `/case-studies`
- Does not link to `/automation`, `/proven-results`, `/locations`, or `/team`
- Blog and news sections link to individual posts
- No prominent link to `/book-a-demo`

## UX Observations

- Long page with multiple sections — well-structured visual hierarchy
- Hero section is bold and conversion-focused
- Pricing section on homepage may reduce need to visit `/pricing` (potential analytics drop-off)
- AI chatbot widget present for lead capture
- Mobile responsive

## Content Observations

- Strong headline and subheadline
- Stats (500+ campaigns, $10M ad spend, 3× ROI) are prominent trust signals
- FAQ section adds keyword weight
- Content is US English — needs conversion to British English per brand standard
- "centers" used instead of "centres" in meta description

## SEO Observations

- **Title tag:** "We market healthcare. Relentlessly." — not keyword-optimised. Does not include "healthcare marketing agency" or geographic modifier.
- **Meta description:** "Premium digital marketing for ERs, MedSpas, and urgent care centers." — short, could be stronger. Uses US spelling "centers".
- H1 is branded/emotive rather than keyword-targeted
- No `LocalBusiness` or `Organization` schema detected
- No `FAQPage` schema despite visible FAQ section

## Metadata Observations

- Title tag is too branded, not enough keyword signal
- Meta description lacks geographic targeting and primary keywords
- Open Graph tags likely present via Next.js metadata but not verified

## What Is Missing

- Primary commercial keyword in title tag and H1
- Geographic modifier (Texas / Dallas / Houston)
- `LocalBusiness` schema
- `FAQPage` schema
- `Organization` schema
- Links to `/automation`, `/proven-results`, `/locations`
- British English throughout

## What Should Be Improved

- Title tag should include "Healthcare Marketing Agency" and a geographic signal
- Meta description should be expanded with keyword targeting and urgency
- H1 should balance brand voice with keyword inclusion
- Add structured data for FAQ, LocalBusiness, Organization
- Add internal links to Automation, Proven Results, and Locations
- Convert all copy to British English

## Priority Level

**Critical** — homepage is the primary ranking and conversion page.
