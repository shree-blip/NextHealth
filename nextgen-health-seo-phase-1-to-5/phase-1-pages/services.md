- Team leadership section may be better suited to `/about` or `/team`

## SEO Observations

- Title tag "Healthcare Marketing Services" is well-targeted
- Meta description is strong and keyword-rich
- H1 aligns with primary keyword
- Missing `Service` schema
- No breadcrumb navigation
- Risk of keyword cannibalisation with individual service pages if not properly structured

## Metadata Observations

- Title tag is solid
- Meta description could include a geographic modifier
- H2 hierarchy is logical

## What Is Missing

- `Service` schema
- `BreadcrumbList` schema
- Cross-links to case studies showing service results
- Cross-links to automation page
- Clear visual separation between overview section and deep-content sections

## What Should Be Improved

- Add geographic modifier to meta description
- Add structured data (`Service`, `BreadcrumbList`)
- Cross-link case studies within each service section
- Remove or relocate team leadership section to appropriate page
- Rename "Vibe Coding" to clearer terminology
- Ensure British English throughout
