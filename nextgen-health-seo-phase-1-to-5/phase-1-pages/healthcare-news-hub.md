## Purpose

Aggregates healthcare industry news to position the brand as an informed industry voice. Supports topical authority and freshness signals.

"Industry News & Updates"

Seven news articles covering healthcare SEO trends, HIPAA-compliant automation tools, healthcare grand opening events, telehealth marketing, GBP updates, Google Ads policy changes, and healthcare workforce shortage. Categorised as Technology News, Industry Report, and Regulatory Update. Dated January–March 2026.

## Current Primary Topic

Healthcare industry news and regulatory updates.

## Current CTA

"Read Our Blog" / "Contact Us"

---

## Internal Linking Observations

- Links to individual news articles
- CTA links to `/blog` and `/contact`
- No cross-links to service pages or automation page
- No sidebar navigation

## UX Observations

- Clean card layout similar to blog hub
- Category labels visible on cards (Technology News, Industry Report, Regulatory Update)
- Newsletter signup CTA at bottom
- No search or filtering

## Content Observations

- Only seven articles — thin volume
- Two articles have "2023" in their titles despite being published in 2026 — significant credibility issue
- Good topic diversity
- Content serves brand authority but may cannibalise blog content if not properly differentiated

## SEO Observations

- H1 "Industry News & Updates" is acceptable but generic
- Title tag matches H1
- Meta description is solid
- No `NewsArticle` or `CollectionPage` schema
- Outdated year references in titles/slugs damage CTR and trust
- Potential cannibalisation risk with blog content on overlapping topics (e.g. HIPAA tools, SEO trends)

## Metadata Observations

- Title and meta description are adequate
- Could include "Healthcare" and "Marketing" keywords more explicitly

## What Is Missing

- `CollectionPage` schema
- Category filtering
- Proper date management (fix 2023 references)
- Differentiation strategy from blog content
- Cross-links to relevant service pages
- More frequent publishing cadence
- Breadcrumb navigation
- British English

## What Should Be Improved

- Fix articles with "2023" in titles and slugs (301 redirect old slugs)
- Change H1 to "Healthcare Industry News" for clearer keyword targeting
- Add category filtering
- Establish clear editorial distinction from blog (news = industry reporting; blog = actionable strategy)
- Add structured data
- Increase publishing frequency
- Convert to British English

## Priority Level

**Medium** — supports authority and freshness but lower conversion potential than blog.
