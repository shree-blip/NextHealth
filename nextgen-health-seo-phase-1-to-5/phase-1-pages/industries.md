# Industries Page

**URL:** `https://thenextgenhealth.com/industries`
**Page Type:** Industry Hub
**Status:** Live

---

## Purpose

Showcases vertical-specific marketing expertise for three core healthcare verticals. Intended to attract industry-specific search traffic and demonstrate specialist knowledge.

## Current H1

"Healthcare Industries We Serve"

## Summary of Current Content

Details tailored marketing approaches for Freestanding ERs (zero-click search dominance, high-acuity keyword bidding, competitor geofencing), Urgent Care (local SEO, broad symptom keywords, aggressive reputation management), and Wellness/Longevity Clinics (Meta ads, email/SMS nurture, AEO and long-form content for elective cash-pay services).

## Current Primary Topic

Industry-specific healthcare marketing strategy.

## Current CTA

"Get a Custom Strategy" → links to `/contact`

## UX Observations

## Content Observations

- Excellent strategic depth per vertical
- Content could be expanded into three dedicated sub-pages without duplication
- Missing dental, mental health, and primary care verticals referenced elsewhere on the site
- "Wellness & Longevity Clinics" is broad — could be segmented into MedSpa and functional medicine

## SEO Observations

- H1 is generic — "Healthcare Industries We Serve" lacks specificity
- Missing industry-specific long-tail keywords in headings
- No `FAQPage` schema despite FAQ section
- No breadcrumbs
- Single-page architecture for all industries wastes keyword targeting

## Metadata Observations

- Title tag covers healthcare engineering well
- Meta description references Texas and specific verticals — solid
- Could include more specific terms like "freestanding ER marketing" or "medspa advertising"

## What Is Missing

- Dedicated sub-pages for each industry vertical
- Links to relevant service pages per vertical
- Links to corresponding case studies
- `FAQPage` schema
- Breadcrumb navigation
- Coverage of dental, mental health, and primary care verticals

## What Should Be Improved

- Create individual industry pages (`/industries/freestanding-ers`, `/industries/urgent-care`, `/industries/medspa`)
- Add cross-links to relevant service pages within each vertical section
- Add links to matching case studies
- Expand verticals to include dental, mental health, and primary care
- Add structured data
- Convert to British English
