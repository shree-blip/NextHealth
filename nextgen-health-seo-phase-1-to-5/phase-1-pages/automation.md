## Purpose

Promotes free downloadable N8N workflow templates for healthcare practices. Serves as both a service showcase and a lead generation magnet.

Covers the problem of administrative bloat (missed calls, staff burnout, data entry errors), offers six plug-and-play N8N templates (Patient Intake, Appointment Reminders, Google Review Collection, Insurance Verification, AI Chatbot Lead Capture, Social Media Auto-Poster), and showcases real-world use cases. Key stats: 40% no-show reduction, 15–20 hrs/week staff time saved, 3× review collection rate.
