# Pricing Page

**URL:** `https://thenextgenhealth.com/pricing`
**Page Type:** Commercial
**Status:** Live

---

## Purpose

Presents three pricing tiers and quantifies ROI of services. Moves prospects from consideration to decision by making investment clear and justified.

## Current H1

"Healthcare Marketing Pricing"

## Summary of Current Content

Three tiers: Starter Care ($5,000/mo — SEO, GBP, Ads, AI Chatbot, Reports, Content), Growth Pro ($10,000/mo — High-Budget Ads, AI Call Handling, Insurance Bots, Priority SLA, Multi-Location, 24/7 Monitoring, Dedicated Account Manager), and Scale Elite (Custom — Custom Software, HIPAA Integrations, Multi-State, BI Dashboards, White-Glove Onboarding). Quantifies automation ROI: 15+ hours saved weekly, 300% net ROI. FAQ section included.

## Current Primary Topic

Healthcare marketing pricing and ROI.

## Current CTA

"Get started" → links to `/signup` per tier / "Schedule a Consultation"

---

## Internal Linking Observations

- Links to `/signup` per tier
- Links to `/contact` for custom pricing
- Does not link to `/services` detail pages to explain what each tier includes
- Does not link to `/case-studies` to justify pricing with results

## UX Observations

- Three-tier structure is clean and scannable
- ROI quantification section strengthens value proposition
- FAQ section addresses common objections
- "Get started" links to signup rather than a consultation — may create friction for high-ticket services

## Content Observations

- Pricing is transparent — strong differentiator in healthcare marketing
- Per-tier feature lists are detailed
- Missing comparison with competitor pricing or industry benchmarks
- Missing money-back guarantee or risk-reversal language
- ROI section is strong

## SEO Observations

- H1 "Healthcare Marketing Pricing" is well-targeted
- Meta description is excellent
- No `FAQPage` schema despite FAQ section
- No `Product` or `Offer` schema for pricing tiers
- Risk of thin content if search engines primarily index the pricing table

## Metadata Observations

- Title and meta description are strong
- Could benefit from including "plans" or "packages" in title for broader keyword capture

## What Is Missing

- `FAQPage` schema
- `Offer` or `Product` schema for tiers
- Links to service detail pages per feature
- Links to case studies per tier
- Risk-reversal language (guarantee, cancellation policy)
- Breadcrumb navigation
- British English

## What Should Be Improved

- Add structured data for FAQ and pricing tiers
- Link each feature to its service detail page
- Add case study references per tier
- Add risk-reversal messaging
- Consider changing "Get started" CTA to "Schedule a Call" for high-ticket tiers
- Convert to British English

## Priority Level

**High** — direct revenue page.
