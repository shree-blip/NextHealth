# Case Studies Page

**URL:** `https://thenextgenhealth.com/case-studies`
**Page Type:** Social Proof
**Status:** Live

---

## Purpose

Showcases client success stories to build trust and demonstrate capability. Converts consideration-stage prospects by providing evidence of results.

## Current H1

"Healthcare Marketing Case Studies"

## Summary of Current Content

Six client success stories: ER Network (45% patient increase), Urgent Care (3× patient acquisition), Cosmetic Surgery (120% lead growth), Primary Care (500% SEO ROI), Mental Health Clinic (2× patient retention), and Dental Practice (#1 Local Pack ranking). Each includes challenge, solution, and results. Real dashboard analytics from Google Search Console and Google My Business are displayed.

## Current Primary Topic

Proven healthcare marketing results.

## Current CTA

"Get Similar Results" / "Schedule Free Consultation" → links to `/contact`

## Internal Linking Observations

- Links to `/contact`
- Does not link to relevant service pages per case study
- Does not link to related blog posts
- Does not link to `/proven-results` (overlapping content)

## UX Observations

- All six case studies on a single page — long scroll
- Dashboard screenshots add authenticity
- Challenge/solution/results format is effective
- No way to navigate or filter by industry

## Content Observations

- Six case studies provide decent proof breadth
- No individual case study pages — wastes keyword targeting opportunity
- Content overlaps significantly with `/proven-results`
- Missing specific service details used per case study
- Missing client testimonials or quotes within case studies

## SEO Observations

- H1 is well-targeted: "Healthcare Marketing Case Studies"
- Meta description is strong
- No `Article` or `Review` schema
- Single-page architecture prevents targeting industry-specific case study keywords
- Potential cannibalisation with `/proven-results`

## Metadata Observations

- Title and meta description are solid
- Could include industry keywords for richer SERP display

## What Is Missing

- Individual case study pages for each client story
- Links to relevant service pages per case study
- Client testimonials/quotes
- `Article` or `CaseStudy` schema
- Breadcrumb navigation
- Industry filtering
- Differentiation from `/proven-results`
- British English

## What Should Be Improved

- Create individual case study pages (6 pages)
- Add service page cross-links within each case study
- Add client quotes/testimonials
- Add structured data
- Establish clear differentiation: case studies = narrative stories; proven results = metrics dashboard
- Convert to British English

## Priority Level

**High** — critical for conversion and trust.
