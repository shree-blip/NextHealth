# Contact Page

**URL:** `https://thenextgenhealth.com/contact`
**Page Type:** Lead Capture
**Status:** Live

---

## Purpose

Primary conversion endpoint. Functions as a multi-step qualification form rather than a traditional contact page.

## Current H1

"High Conversion Qualification"

## Summary of Current Content

Multi-step qualification form asking prospects to select their facility type (Freestanding ER, Urgent Care, Wellness Clinic, Multi-Specialty) across four steps. Positions NexHealth as selective, partnering only with financially viable medical practices ready to scale.

## Current Primary Topic

Lead qualification and capture.

## Current CTA

"Start Qualification" / "Next Step" (multi-step form)

---

## Internal Linking Observations

- Minimal outbound links — form-focused page
- Not linked from many internal pages beyond CTAs
- Does not include secondary conversion paths (phone number, email, chat)

## UX Observations

- Multi-step form is engaging but may create friction for users expecting a simple contact form
- "Qualification" language may feel exclusionary to smaller practices
- No phone number or email visible for users who prefer direct contact
- No map or address shown

## Content Observations

- Very thin indexable content — primarily a form
- No trust signals on the page (testimonials, client logos, guarantees)
- No supporting copy explaining what happens after submission
- H1 "High Conversion Qualification" is internal jargon, not user-facing language

## SEO Observations

- H1 is not keyword-optimised — "Contact" or "Get in Touch" would be more natural
- Meta description positions selectivity but does not include relevant keywords
- Title tag "High Conversion Qualification" is not SEO-friendly
- No `ContactPoint` or `LocalBusiness` schema
- Thin content reduces ranking potential for "healthcare marketing contact" queries

## Metadata Observations

- Title tag reads as internal terminology, not visitor-friendly
- Meta description is decent but overly sales-focused

## What Is Missing

- Keyword-optimised H1 and title tag
- Phone number, email address, physical address
- Trust signals (logos, testimonials, guarantees)
- "What to expect" section
- Response time commitment
- `LocalBusiness` schema with contact details
- Map embed
- British English

## What Should Be Improved

- Change H1 to something user-facing: "Get Your Healthcare Growth Plan"
- Add supporting conversion copy above and below the form
- Include phone number, email address, and physical address
- Add trust signals
- Add `ContactPoint` and `LocalBusiness` schema
- Convert to British English

## Priority Level

**Critical** — primary conversion page. Every CTA on the site points here.
