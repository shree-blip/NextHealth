## Internal Linking Observations

- Links to `/contact` for applications
- Does not link to `/about`, `/services`, or `/case-studies`

## UX Observations

- Clean team grid layout
- Professional photos and role titles
- "The Infrastructure" as H1 is off-brand for a team page

## Content Observations

- Eight team members provide good team visibility
- Missing individual bios, expertise areas, or credentials
- "Join the Mission" recruitment section is well-positioned
- H1 "The Infrastructure" is abstract — may confuse visitors looking for a team page

## SEO Observations

- H1 "The Infrastructure" is not keyword-optimised — should include "team"
- Low direct SEO value but important for E-E-A-T
- No `Person` or `Organization` schema
- Missing author bio pages (important for E-E-A-T on blog/news content)

## What Is Missing

- Keyword-optimised H1 (e.g. "Our Healthcare Marketing Team")
- Individual bios and expertise areas
- Links to team members' authored content
- `Person` schema per team member
- Links to `/about`, `/services`
- Author bio pages for content writers
- British English

## What Should Be Improved

- Change H1 to "Our Healthcare Marketing Team"
- Add individual bios with expertise and credentials
- Add `Person` schema
- Create author pages that link to their blog/news content
- Cross-link to About and Services
- Convert to British English

## Priority Level

**Medium** — important for E-E-A-T, especially for content authorship.
