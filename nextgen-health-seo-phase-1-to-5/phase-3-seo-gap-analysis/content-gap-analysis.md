# Content Gap Analysis

**Site:** thenextgenhealth.com
**Audit Date:** 8 April 2026

---

## Executive Summary

The NextGen Health website has **significant content gaps** that limit its ability to rank for high-value keywords and convert visitors into leads. The site's 44 indexable pages are insufficient for a full-service healthcare marketing agency competing in a national market with local (Texas) roots. Key gaps include: zero industry vertical pages, zero automation template pages, zero individual case studies, zero city pages, thin blog volume (7 posts), and minimal E-E-A-T signals (no author bios, no credentials).

**Current Content Volume:** 44 indexable pages
**Recommended Content Volume (12-month target):** 120–150 indexable pages
**Content Deficit:** 76–106 pages

## 3. E-E-A-T Content Gaps — **CRITICAL FOR YMYL**

Healthcare marketing is a YMYL (Your Money Your Life) adjacent topic. Google requires strong E-E-A-T signals.

| E-E-A-T Signal | Current State | Gap | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Experience** — Evidence of first-hand experience | Case study hub (no detail pages) | ❌ No demonstrated experience with data | P0 |
| **Expertise** — Author credentials | No author bios or attribution | ❌ Zero expertise signals on content | P0 |
| **Authoritativeness** — Industry recognition | No awards, certifications, or press mentions visible | ❌ Thin authority signals | P1 |
| **Trustworthiness** — Reviews, HIPAA, security | HIPAA page exists; no reviews displayed | ⚠️ Partial — needs client testimonials | P1 |

### Recommended E-E-A-T Content Additions

1. **Author pages** with bios, headshots, credentials, and links to authored content
2. **Individual case studies** with real data (anonymised if needed)
3. **Client testimonials** with full name and practice
4. **Certifications & awards section** on about page
5. **Published methodology** explaining approach to healthcare marketing
6. **Press/media mentions** page (if applicable)
7. **Professional affiliations** (HCMA, AMA marketing partnerships, etc.)
