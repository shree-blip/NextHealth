**Audit Date:** 8 April 2026

> Per-page gap analysis for all 30 live core/service pages. Blog and news articles are assessed in the Content Gap Analysis.

## Scoring Key

Each page is scored 1–5 across five dimensions:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Critical gap — missing entirely |
| 2 | Major gap — present but severely lacking |
| 3 | Moderate gap — functional but needs improvement |
| 4 | Minor gap — good, small optimisation needed |
| 5 | Excellent — no meaningful gap |

**Dimensions:** Title Tag | Meta Description | H1 / Headings | Content Depth | Internal Links
