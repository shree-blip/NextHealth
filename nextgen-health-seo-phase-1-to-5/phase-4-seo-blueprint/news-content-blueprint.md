## Universal News Article Template

### Front Matter

```
Title: [Timely, keyword-rich, 50–60 chars]
Meta Description: [155 chars, timely, with keyword]
Slug: /news/[descriptive-slug]  (NO year in slug unless annual roundup)
Author: [Team member name]
Published: [Date]
Updated: [Date]
Category: [Industry News / Policy Update / Technology / Trends / Strategy]
Tags: [3–5 tags]
```

### Required Elements

1. **Breadcrumb** — Home > News > [Article Title]
2. **Author byline** with headshot and link to author page
3. **Published + Updated dates** — visible
4. **NewsArticle schema** — JSON-LD
5. **Internal links** — Min 2 to service/core pages
6. **Related articles** — 3 at bottom
7. **CTA** — Contextual CTA near bottom

### Content Format by Type

| Article Type | Word Count | Frequency | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short news update | 400–600 | Weekly | What happened → why it matters → what to do |
| Analysis piece | 800–1,200 | Fortnightly | Background → analysis → implications → recommendation |
| Comprehensive report | 1,500+ | Monthly | Full deep-dive with data, charts, expert commentary |

## News Content Quality Standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Timeliness | Reference current dates, recent data, recent events |
| Sourcing | Cite primary sources: Google announcements, CMS, FTC, PubMed, Pew Research |
| Objectivity | Present facts first, then NextGen Health's perspective |
| Actionability | Every article ends with "what to do about it" |
| British English | Throughout |
| HIPAA accuracy | All compliance references verified |
| Unique angle | Relate industry news back to healthcare marketing specifically |
| Freshness signals | Update articles when new information becomes available |
