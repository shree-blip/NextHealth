# Service Pages SEO Blueprint

**Site:** thenextgenhealth.com
**Blueprint Date:** 8 April 2026

> Unified blueprint for all 13 service detail pages.

---

## Universal Service Page Template

Every service page should follow this structure:

### Required Sections

1. **Breadcrumb** — `Home > Services > [Service Name]`
2. **H1** — Contains "Healthcare" + service keyword
3. **Hero Section** — Value proposition + primary CTA + supporting image/graphic
4. **Overview** — What this service is and why it matters for healthcare (200–300 words)
5. **What We Do** — 4–6 specific deliverables with brief descriptions
6. **Our Process** — 3–5 step process (How We Work)
7. **Results / Proof** — Mini case study or key stat
8. **FAQ** — 3–5 questions (with FAQ schema)
9. **Related Services** — 3–5 complementary services
10. **Blog / Resources** — 2–3 related blog posts
11. **HIPAA Trust Statement** — One-line + link to `/hipaa`
12. **CTA** — "Book a Free Demo" or "Get a Free Strategy Session"

### Required Schema
- Service schema (name, description, provider, areaServed)
- FAQ schema (for FAQ section)
- BreadcrumbList schema

### Content Standards
- Minimum 1,200 words; target 1,500–2,000
- Primary keyword in H1, first paragraph, and at least 2 H2s
- 2–4 images per page (with descriptive alt text)
- Internal links: 8+ outbound
