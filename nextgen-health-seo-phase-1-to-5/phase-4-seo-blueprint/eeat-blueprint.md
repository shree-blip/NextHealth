# E-E-A-T Blueprint

**Site:** thenextgenhealth.com
**Blueprint Date:** 8 April 2026

> Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — Google's quality framework for evaluating content credibility. Critical for YMYL-adjacent healthcare marketing content.

---

## Why E-E-A-T Matters for NextGen Health

Healthcare marketing sits adjacent to YMYL (Your Money Your Life) topics. While NextGen Health doesn't provide medical advice directly, its content:
- Advises on healthcare business decisions (financial impact)
- References HIPAA compliance (regulatory/legal)
- Discusses patient acquisition (impacts healthcare access)
- Covers healthcare advertising regulations

Google holds YMYL-adjacent content to a **higher standard** for E-E-A-T signals.

## 2. Expertise — Demonstrate Subject Matter Knowledge

### Current Gaps
- No author attribution on any content
- No author bio pages
- No credentials displayed
- No individual expertise areas highlighted

### Author Attribution System

**Step 1:** Create author pages under `/team/[slug]`

Each author page must include:
- Full name and professional photo
- Role at NextGen Health
- Areas of expertise (tags)
- Professional background (relevant experience)
- Certifications and qualifications
- Links to all authored content
- LinkedIn profile link
- Person schema markup

**Step 2:** Add author byline to every blog post and news article

```
Written by [Name], [Role] at NextGen Health
[Photo] [Bio snippet] [Link to author page]
Published [date] | Updated [date]
```

**Step 3:** Add Person schema to each author page (see Schema Blueprint)

## 5. E-E-A-T Content Audit Checklist

Use this checklist for every content page:

### Experience Signals
- [ ] Does the content demonstrate first-hand experience?
- [ ] Are there real examples, case studies, or data from actual projects?
- [ ] Does the author have demonstrated experience in this topic?

### Expertise Signals
- [ ] Is the author clearly identified with a byline?
- [ ] Does the author have a bio page with relevant credentials?
- [ ] Is the content technically accurate and comprehensive?
- [ ] Does the content go deeper than a generic overview?

### Authority Signals
- [ ] Is the content published on a site with relevant authority?
- [ ] Are there external references or citations?
- [ ] Does the page link to other authoritative content on the site?
- [ ] Are there signs of industry recognition (awards, partnerships)?

### Trust Signals
- [ ] Is the business clearly identified with NAP data?
- [ ] Is there a way to contact the business easily?
- [ ] Are there client reviews or testimonials?
- [ ] Is the site secure (HTTPS)?
- [ ] Are privacy and terms policies accessible?
- [ ] Is the content current (not outdated)?
- [ ] Are HIPAA/compliance claims accurate?
