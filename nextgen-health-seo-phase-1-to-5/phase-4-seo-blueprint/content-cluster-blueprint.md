---

## What Is a Content Cluster?

A content cluster is a group of related pages organised around a **pillar page** (broad topic) supported by **cluster content** (specific subtopics) connected through **strategic internal links**. This architecture signals topical authority to Google and helps users navigate related content.

```
         [Pillar Page]
        /      |       \
  [Cluster]  [Cluster]  [Cluster]
      |         |          |
  [Sub-topic] [Sub-topic] [Sub-topic]
```
