## Channel-by-Channel Playbook

### 1. Guest Posting — P0

**Target Publications:**

| Tier | Examples | DA Range | Monthly Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (industry) | Healthcare IT News, Becker's, Medical Economics | 60+ | 1 |
| Tier 2 (marketing) | Search Engine Journal, HubSpot, Content Marketing Institute | 50–70 | 1–2 |
| Tier 3 (niche) | DentistryIQ, Aesthetic Guide, Practice Builders blog | 30–50 | 2–3 |

**Process:**
1. Identify target publication + editorial guidelines
2. Pitch 3 topic ideas per publication (healthcare marketing focus)
3. Write 1,000–1,500 word article with 1–2 contextual links back to site
4. Include author bio linking to `/about` or `/team`
5. Promote published article on social channels

**Link Targets (where to point guest post links):**

| Priority | Target Page | Anchor Text Variations |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | `/services/search-engine-optimisation` | healthcare SEO, medical practice SEO, SEO for clinics |
| P0 | `/services/google-ads` | healthcare PPC, Google Ads for doctors |
| P1 | `/industries/dental` (once live) | dental marketing, dental SEO |
| P1 | `/blog/[keyword-rich-post]` | descriptive / natural anchors |
| P2 | `/case-studies/[slug]` | "see this case study", "[industry] results" |

**Anchor Text Distribution:**

| Type | Target % |
|---|---|
| Branded ("NextGen Health") | 30–35% |
| Natural / Naked URL | 20–25% |
| Partial Match | 20–25% |
| Exact Match | 10–15% |
| Generic ("click here", "learn more") | 5–10% |

### 2. Digital PR — P0

**Linkable Asset Creation:**

Create 1 major linkable asset per quarter:

| Quarter | Asset | Format | Target Links |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q2 2026 | "State of Healthcare Marketing 2026" | Research report / infographic | 20–30 |
| Q3 2026 | "Healthcare Patient Acquisition Cost Benchmarks" | Interactive data tool | 15–25 |
| Q4 2026 | "Healthcare Website Speed Index" | Benchmark study | 15–20 |
| Q1 2027 | "AI in Healthcare Marketing Survey" | Report + press release | 25–40 |

**Outreach Process:**
1. Create data-driven asset with unique research / analysis
2. Design shareable infographic or interactive element
3. Write press release (for Tier 1 assets)
4. Build media list: 50–100 journalists / bloggers per asset
5. Personalised outreach via email (BuzzStream or Pitchbox)
6. Follow up 3× over 2 weeks
7. Track placements and links acquired

**PR Distribution Channels:**
- PRWeb / PR Newswire (for major reports)
- Industry-specific wire services (healthcare)
- Direct journalist outreach (HARO, Twitter/X, LinkedIn)

### 3. HARO / Expert Commentary — P1

**Platforms:**
- HARO (Help a Reporter Out) — Daily monitoring
- Qwoted — Healthcare / marketing categories
- Terkel — Expert roundup contributions
- SourceBottle — Australian/international queries

**Process:**
1. Set up alerts for: "healthcare marketing", "medical practice", "patient acquisition", "dental marketing", "healthcare SEO"
2. Respond to 3–5 relevant queries per week
3. Provide concise, expert commentary with credentials
4. Include link to relevant page when permitted
5. Track published mentions and follow up for link inclusion

**Response Template:**
> [Expert name], [Title] at NextGen Health, says: "[Concise expert answer in 2–3 sentences]."
>
> "[Supporting detail or data point in 1–2 sentences]."

**Expected Conversion:** 10–15% of submissions result in published mention.

---

### 4. Resource Page Link Building — P1

**Target Resource Pages:**
- University healthcare programme resource lists
- Healthcare association member resource pages
- Marketing tool resource roundups
- Small business resource directories

**Process:**
1. Search queries: `"healthcare marketing" + "resources"`, `"dental marketing" + "useful links"`, `intitle:resources "healthcare" "marketing"`
2. Evaluate page DA and relevance
3. Identify specific content on our site that fits the resource list
4. Personalised outreach to webmaster / page owner
5. Suggest specific page + anchor text

**Target:** 2–4 resource page links per month.

---

### 5. Broken Link Building — P1

**Process:**
1. Use Ahrefs / Screaming Frog to find broken links on target sites (healthcare marketing blogs, industry publications)
2. Identify broken links pointing to content we have (or can create) an equivalent for
3. Contact webmaster: "I noticed a broken link on your page. We have a similar resource at [URL]."
4. Offer our content as a replacement

**Focus Areas:**
- Healthcare marketing industry blogs with outdated links
- Competitor backlink profiles (find their broken/lost links)
- University and .edu resource pages

---

### 6. Unlinked Brand Mention Reclamation — P1

**Setup:**
1. Set up Google Alerts for: "NextGen Health", "thenextgenhealth", "NextGen Health marketing"
2. Use Ahrefs Content Explorer to find unlinked mentions
3. Use BrandMentions or Mention.com for real-time monitoring

**Process:**
1. Identify mention without link
2. Contact author / webmaster
3. Politely request link addition: "Thanks for mentioning us! Would you mind adding a link to [URL] so your readers can find us easily?"
4. Track conversion rate

**Expected Conversion:** 20–30% of unlinked mentions convert to links.

## Link Quality Guidelines

### Acceptable Links

- DA 20+ (exceptions for highly relevant niche sites)
- Contextual, within body content
- From real websites with organic traffic
- Relevant to healthcare, marketing, or business
- Dofollow preferred; nofollow from high-DA sites acceptable

### Reject / Disavow

- PBN (private blog network) links
- Paid link schemes
- Irrelevant / spammy directories
- Sites with DA <10 and no traffic
- Sites with thin or AI-generated content farms
- Sitewide footer / sidebar links from low-quality sites
