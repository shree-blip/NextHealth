# Industry Pages SEO Blueprint

**Site:** thenextgenhealth.com
**Blueprint Date:** 8 April 2026

> All 10 recommended industry vertical pages — to be created under `/industries/[slug]`.

---

## Universal Industry Page Template

### Required Sections

1. **Breadcrumb** — `Home > Industries > [Industry Name]`
2. **H1** — `[Industry] Marketing — Grow Your [Practice Type]`
3. **Hero** — Industry-specific imagery + value proposition + CTA
4. **Industry Challenges** — 4–6 challenges specific to this vertical
5. **Our Services for [Industry]** — Curated list linking to relevant service pages
6. **Results / Case Study Snippet** — Data from relevant case study (or aggregate stats until created)
7. **Why Choose NextGen Health for [Industry]** — Differentiators for this vertical
8. **FAQ** — 4–6 industry-specific questions (with FAQ schema)
9. **Related Industries** — 2–3 links to other industry pages
10. **CTA** — "Book a Free [Industry] Marketing Demo"

### Required Schema
- Service schema (specific to this industry)
- FAQ schema
- BreadcrumbList

### Content Standards
- Minimum 1,500 words; target 2,000–2,500
- Industry-specific language and terminology throughout
- 3–5 internal links to relevant service pages
- 2–3 images specific to industry
