## Content Requirements

| Section | Purpose | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Convert visitors | Clear value prop, primary CTA (Book a Demo), secondary CTA (See Results) |
| Services overview | Navigation + keyword density | 3×4 grid linking to all 13 services with brief descriptions |
| Industries overview | Cross-sell + new keyword targets | Cards for each industry vertical (when pages are created) |
| Why us | Trust + differentiation | HIPAA compliance, proven results, automation technology |
| Results snapshot | Social proof | 3–5 key metrics from proven results page |
| Testimonials | Trust signals | 2–3 client quotes with attribution |
| Blog preview | Freshness signal | 3 latest blog posts with thumbnails |
| CTA section | Conversion | "Book a Free Demo" with form or Calendly embed |
